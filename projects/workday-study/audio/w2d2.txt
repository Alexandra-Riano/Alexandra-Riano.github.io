Week 2, day 2. Position Management. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Position Management Options, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Control persistent individual positions, restrictions, vacancies, and history. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] A position exists independently from its incumbent. An open, approved position is required to hire, promote, demote, or transfer a worker. Restrictions apply to an individual position, and a position stays open until it is filled or manually closed. You can report on open and filled positions and on position history, assign company, cost center, and region to unfilled positions, and move positions between organizations. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Best fit: Individual seat and vacancy control. [[slnc 700]] Restriction level: Individual position. [[slnc 700]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] A hospital must control and report every approved nursing seat, including vacancies and position history. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Create Position, Edit Position Restrictions, and Maintain Staffing Models. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] Do not choose Job Management when the scenario requires persistent vacancy or position-level history. Once positions are created, Edit Staffing Model is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
