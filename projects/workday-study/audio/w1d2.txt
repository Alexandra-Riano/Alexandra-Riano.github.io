Week 1, day 2. Study Job Profiles. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Create  and  Edit Job Profiles, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Define reusable characteristics used by jobs and positions. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Job profiles describe general work characteristics. Normal required fields are name, job code, and effective date unless job-code generation is configured. Optional fields include title, management level, classifications, pay rate type, compensation grade, qualifications, country restriction, and description. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Job profile: Reusable work definition. [[slnc 700]] Job or position: Staffable instance in an organization. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Use one Executive Assistant profile across multiple positions instead of duplicating the definition for each department. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Create Job Profile, Edit Job Profile, Job Catalog, and Edit Tenant Setup - H C M. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] A profile is not a worker and is not the same as a position. Effective-dated edits can affect staffing, eligibility, and reporting. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
