Week 5, day 3. Step types. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Business Process Step Types, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Match initiation, action, approval, service, and to-do behavior to the requirement. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Initiation starts the event and is always first. Action steps either prompt a review, where the reviewer can edit and approve, or start a subprocess. Approval steps approve, send back, or deny the whole process; approvers cannot edit, and a denial denies the entire process. Service steps run Workday-delivered automation such as Create Workday Account. To Do steps send an Inbox reminder for work inside or outside Workday; Workday delivers none, so you create them with Maintain To Dos. Approval chains move up the management chain until the top or an exit condition. A consolidated approval combines several approvals for the same worker into one task, but only for steps before it. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Approval: Formal decision, no editing. [[slnc 700]] Action: A review (with editing) or a subprocess. [[slnc 700]] Service: Workday-delivered automation. [[slnc 700]] To Do: Reminder for work Workday may not check. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Use Approval for a formal approve-or-deny decision; do not substitute a To Do checkbox. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Edit Business Process Definition, Add Step, and View Diagram. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] Completing a To Do proves the click, not necessarily the external activity. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check. I'll read each question and the choices, then pause so you can answer out loud. [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is C: Initiation. [[slnc 500]] Initiation defines who can start the process. [[slnc 1400]] Question two. [[slnc 400]] A manager must review and possibly edit data before HR approves. Which step type fits? [[slnc 700]] A: To Do. [[slnc 450]] B: Service. [[slnc 450]] C: Approval. [[slnc 450]] D: Action (Review). [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is D: Action (Review). [[slnc 500]] Review steps allow editing; approval steps don't. [[slnc 1400]] Question three. [[slnc 400]] What can recipients of an Approval step do? [[slnc 700]] A: Only acknowledge a message. [[slnc 450]] B: Edit any field on the event, then approve it to move it forward. [[slnc 450]] C: Skip the step whenever they like. [[slnc 450]] D: Approve, send back, or deny, but not edit. [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is D: Approve, send back, or deny, but not edit. [[slnc 500]] Approvers can't edit business process data.
