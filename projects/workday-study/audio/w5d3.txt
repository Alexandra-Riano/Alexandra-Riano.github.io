Week 5, day 3. Step types. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Business Process Step Types, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Match initiation, action, approval, service, and to-do behavior to the requirement. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Initiation starts the event; Action performs Workday work or a subprocess; Approval records a decision; Service invokes automation; To Do instructs a person to complete work that Workday might not validate. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Approval: Formal decision. [[slnc 700]] Action: Task, review, or subprocess. [[slnc 700]] Service: Automation. [[slnc 700]] To Do: Human follow-up. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Use Approval for a formal approve-or-deny decision; do not substitute a To Do checkbox. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Edit Business Process Definition, Add Step, and View Business Process Diagram. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] Completing a To Do proves the click, not necessarily the external activity. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check. I'll read each question and the choices, then pause so you can answer out loud. [[slnc 1000]] Question one. [[slnc 400]] Which step is always first and can't be removed? [[slnc 700]] A: Action. [[slnc 450]] B: Service. [[slnc 450]] C: Approval. [[slnc 450]] D: Initiation. [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is D: Initiation. [[slnc 500]] Initiation defines who can start the process. [[slnc 1400]] Question two. [[slnc 400]] A manager must review and possibly edit data before HR approves. Which step type fits? [[slnc 700]] A: Service. [[slnc 450]] B: Approval. [[slnc 450]] C: Action (Review). [[slnc 450]] D: To Do. [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is C: Action (Review). [[slnc 500]] Review steps allow editing; approval steps don't. [[slnc 1400]] Question three. [[slnc 400]] What can recipients of an Approval step do? [[slnc 700]] A: Skip the step whenever they like. [[slnc 450]] B: Edit any field and then approve. [[slnc 450]] C: Only acknowledge a message. [[slnc 450]] D: Approve, send back, or deny, but not edit. [[slnc 450]] Take a moment. [[slnc 5000]] The answer is D: Approve, send back, or deny, but not edit. [[slnc 500]] Approvers can't edit business process data. [[slnc 1400]]  [[slnc 800]] End of week 5, day 3.
