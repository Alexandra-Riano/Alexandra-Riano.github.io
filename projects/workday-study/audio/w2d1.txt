Week 2, day 1. Job Management. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Job Management Options, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Use organization-level restrictions for flexible, high-volume staffing. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Job Management emphasizes speed and flexibility. Restrictions apply across the supervisory organization, there is no system-imposed position count, and the job instance ends when the worker leaves. Reporting focuses on current headcount. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Best fit: Flexible or high-volume staffing. [[slnc 700]] Restriction level: Supervisory organization. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] A support center hires many agents under shared job, location, worker-type, subtype, and time-type restrictions.
