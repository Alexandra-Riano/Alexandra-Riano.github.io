Week 7, day 3. Security framework. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Configurable Security Framework, in the Security domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Trace access from user to group to policy to secured item. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Users become members of security groups. Groups receive permissions through domain or business-process policies. Domains group securable tasks, reports, data sources, fields, and integrations. business process policies govern participation in event types. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Domain policy: View or modify secured items. [[slnc 700]] business process policy: Initiate or act on events. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] When a user cannot run a report, trace its securing domain, permitted groups, and the user’s membership. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: View Security for Securable Item, Security Analysis for Securable Item and Account, and Domain Security Policies for Functional Area. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] Creating a group or assigning a role does not grant access until a relevant policy gives permission. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
