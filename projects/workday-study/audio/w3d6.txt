Week 3, day 6. Change Job. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Change Job, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Configure promotions, transfers, demotions, lateral moves, and location changes. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Change Job supports promotions, demotions, transfers, lateral moves, and changes to position data and location. Managers usually use Start Job Change, which asks simple questions from a change job template. Administrators use Transfer, Promote or Change Job, which shows a guided editor whose sections are secured by domain. Both run the Change Job business process. Maintain Change Job Templates sets the fields, sections, subprocesses, reasons, and help text for templates on Change Job, Change Location, Request Transfer, and Change Contingent Worker Details. Maintain Event Categories and Reasons maps your reasons to the delivered change types: Transfer, Promotion, Lateral Move, Demotion, and Data Change. Moving a manager can move all of their eligible teams or none, each through its own Assign Superior subprocess. Assign Roles - Change Assignments for Worker reviews roles, and it is not rescinded if Change Job is rescinded. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Start Job Change: Manager task with simple template questions. [[slnc 700]] Transfer, Promote or Change Job: Administrator task with the full guided editor. [[slnc 700]] Request Transfer: For a group without direct access to the worker. [[slnc 700]] Routing modifier: Sends a step to the current or proposed group member. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] A manager wants to bring a worker from another organization onto their team. They have no access to that worker, so they use the Request Transfer micro-task; the receiving manager and HR Partner then review it. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Start Job Change, Transfer, Promote or Change Job, Maintain Change Job Templates, Maintain Event Categories and Reasons, and Configure Consolidated Template. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] Change Job uses a template, so a group needs both the business process security policy (to start or act) and the domain policies (to see each section). Rescinding Change Job does not undo new role assignments. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check. I'll read each question and the choices, then pause so you can answer out loud. [[slnc 1000]] Question one.
