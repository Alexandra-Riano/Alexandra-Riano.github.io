[[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Master the exam-heavy distinction between control and flexibility. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Management Options. [[slnc 500]] Job Management emphasizes speed and flexibility. No open position is required to hire. Restrictions apply across the supervisory organization, there is no system-imposed limit on jobs, and the job ends when the worker leaves. Reporting covers current headcount only, so control relies on hire, contract, and transfer workflows. Subordinate organizations created under a parent inherit its staffing model, but each can be changed, so one hierarchy can mix models. Changing a parent does not change existing subordinates; use Edit Staffing Model on each. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Flexible or high-volume staffing. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Supervisory organization. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Edit Staffing Model works in either direction only until positions exist (Position Management) or hiring restrictions are set (Job Management). After that the task is no longer available. Before changing, rescind in-progress hiring or position restriction events, Move Worker (By Organization) events, and staffing events. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Position Management Options. [[slnc 500]] A position exists independently from its incumbent. An open, approved position is required to hire, promote, demote, or transfer a worker. Restrictions apply to an individual position, and a position stays open until it is filled or manually closed. You can report on open and filled positions and on position history, assign company, cost center, and region to unfilled positions, and move positions between organizations. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Individual seat and vacancy control. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Individual position. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Do not choose Job Management when the scenario requires persistent vacancy or position-level history. Once positions are created, Edit Staffing Model is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1300]] Two retrieval questions to finish. [[slnc 800]] Question one.
