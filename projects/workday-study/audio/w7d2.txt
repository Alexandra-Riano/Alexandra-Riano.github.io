Today's objective is Business Process Notifications, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Communicate progress without granting access or replacing required steps. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Workday sends system notifications, for example when a task arrives in My Tasks. Custom notifications are added to a definition with Add Notification. They trigger on the process status (such as Completed) or when a step is entered or exited, and conditions can be added; the trigger and the condition must both be met. Recipients can be participants, other users, or security groups. Message content mixes text and fields; a field the recipient cannot access shows as [not available]. Receiving a notification never grants access to the process. Custom notifications do not turn off system notifications. Delivery depends on configuration and preferences: immediate email, a daily digest email, and push notifications. Edit Tenant Setup - Notifications holds tenant-wide email and delivery settings. Select Do Not Include Notification Details Link for recipients who have no Workday account. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Notification: Inform someone. [[slnc 700]] business process step: Require or automate work. [[slnc 700]] Trigger: Status, or step entry or exit. [[slnc 700]] System versus custom: Custom ones do not turn system ones off.
