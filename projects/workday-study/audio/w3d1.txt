Week 3, day 1. Staffing business processes. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Staffing Business Processes, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Prepare the organization, then initiate Hire, Change Job, or Termination. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Position Management usually needs an available position before Hire; Job Management relies on organization restrictions. Hire creates the worker relationship, Change Job handles material job or position changes, and Termination ends employment. Each can route subprocesses. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Hire: Bring a worker into an eligible job or position. [[slnc 700]] Change Job: Change job, position, manager, location, or time attributes. [[slnc 700]] Termination: End employment and coordinate downstream work. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] A manager cannot hire into a position-managed organization until a position is available. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Create Position, Set Hiring Restrictions, Hire Employee, Change Job, and Terminate Employee. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] The initiating task is only the start. Also reason about prerequisites, effective dates, routing, security, and subprocesses. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
