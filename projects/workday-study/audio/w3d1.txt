Week 3, day 1. Staffing business processes. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Staffing Business Processes, in the Staffing Setup domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Prepare the organization, then run Hire and its subprocesses. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Prepare first. A Position Management organization needs an open position (Create Position business process); a Job Management organization needs hiring restrictions (Hiring Restrictions business process). Until then, Hire does not appear in the organization's Related Actions. Hire works the same under both models. It records hire date, position, employee type, job profile, time type, and location. Other values default: management level, job classification, and work shift from the job profile, and weekly hours from the location. When a position and its job profile both hold a value, the position wins. A typical Hire definition adds the Propose Compensation Hire and Change Organization Assignments subprocesses, a Review Employee Hire action, a Create Workday Account service step, and To Dos. Change Job and Termination have their own lessons. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Create Position: Prepares a Position Management organization. [[slnc 700]] Hiring Restrictions: Prepares a Job Management organization. [[slnc 700]] Hire: Brings a worker into a position or job. [[slnc 700]] Common Hire steps: Propose Compensation Hire, Change Organization Assignments, Review Employee Hire, Create Workday Account. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] A manager cannot hire into a position-managed organization until a position is available. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Create Position, Set Hiring Restrictions, Hire Employee, Propose Compensation Hire, and Change Organization Assignments for Worker. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] If Hire is missing from an organization's Related Actions, the organization is not prepared: create a position (Position Management) or set hiring restrictions (Job Management).
