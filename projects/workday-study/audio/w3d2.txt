[[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Connect organization preparation to Hire, Change Job, and Termination. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Management Options. [[slnc 500]] Job Management emphasizes speed and flexibility. No open position is required to hire. Hiring restrictions are set on the supervisory organization and apply to every job in it; there is no system-imposed limit on jobs, and the job ends when the worker leaves. Reporting covers current headcount only, so control relies on hire, contract, and transfer workflows. Restriction criteria are the same for both models: job family, job profile, location, worker type, worker sub type, time type, or No Job Restrictions (you must choose at least one). Edit Tenant Setup - H C M can allow users to override restrictions during staffing events; the restriction values still default in. Subordinate organizations inherit their parent's staffing model, but each can be changed, so one hierarchy can mix models. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Flexible or high-volume staffing. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Supervisory organization. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Edit Staffing Model works in either direction only until positions exist (Position Management) or hiring restrictions are set (Job Management). After that the task is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Position Management Options. [[slnc 500]] A position exists independently from its incumbent. An open, approved position is required to hire, promote, demote, or transfer a worker. Restrictions apply to an individual position, and a position stays open until it is filled or manually closed. You can report on open and filled positions and on position history, assign company, cost center, and region to unfilled positions, and move positions between organizations. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Individual seat and vacancy control. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Individual position. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Do not choose Job Management when the scenario requires persistent vacancy or position-level history. Once positions are created, Edit Staffing Model is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Staffing Business Processes. [[slnc 500]] Prepare first. A Position Management organization needs an open position (Create Position business process); a Job Management organization needs hiring restrictions (Hiring Restrictions business process). Until then, Hire does not appear in the organization's Related Actions. Hire works the same under both models. It records hire date, position, employee type, job profile, time type, and location. Other values default: management level, job classification, and work shift from the job profile, and weekly hours from the location. When a position and its job profile both hold a value, the position wins. A typical Hire definition adds the Propose Compensation Hire and Change Organization Assignments subprocesses, a Review Employee Hire action, a Create Workday Account service step, and To Dos. Change Job and Termination have their own lessons. [[slnc 700]] Create Position: Prepares a Position Management organization. [[slnc 500]] Hiring Restrictions: Prepares a Job Management organization. [[slnc 500]] Hire: Brings a worker into a position or job. [[slnc 500]] Common Hire steps: Propose Compensation Hire, Change Organization Assignments, Review Employee Hire, Create Workday Account. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: If Hire is missing from an organization's Related Actions, the organization is not prepared: create a position (Position Management) or set hiring restrictions (Job Management).
