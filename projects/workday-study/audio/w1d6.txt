Week 1, day 6. Navigation, search  and  tenants. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Navigation, Search  and  Tenants, in the Reporting domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Find tasks, objects, and events quickly, and know your tenant types. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] A tenant is one instance of Workday. Production holds live data; Sandbox is a copy for testing; Sandbox Preview adds the next release. Workday metadata cannot change; your configuration and data are tenanted data. Feature releases come twice a year, and service updates arrive weekly. Search shows predictive results after three characters and treats each term separately; Exact Match finds IDs and email addresses. Search prefixes limit results to one object type, for example B P colon for business process definitions, event colon for events, sup: for supervisory organizations, org: for organizations, and worker: for workers; type ? to list all prefixes. Related Actions show what you can do with an object, based on your security. Proxy (Start Proxy) lets testers act as another user, but only outside Production. The Workday Touchpoints Kit shows data flows and configuration relationships across products. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Production versus Sandbox: Live data versus a test copy. [[slnc 700]] B P colon versus event colon: Definitions versus running events. [[slnc 700]] Related Actions: What you can do with an object. [[slnc 700]] Proxy: Testing as another user, never in Production. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work.
