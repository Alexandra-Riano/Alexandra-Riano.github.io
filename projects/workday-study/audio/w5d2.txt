Week 5, day 2. business process definitions. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Update business process Definitions, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Maintain default and organization-specific business process definitions with effective dates. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Default definitions support common routing; organization-specific definitions handle organizations whose steps differ greatly from the default; condition rules on individual steps control whether they run. Workday looks for the initiating organization's definition, then its superior organizations, then the default. The completion step commits data and sets Successfully Completed. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Default: Common workflow for all organizations. [[slnc 700]] Organization-specific: Own steps for a target org and its subordinates. [[slnc 700]] Condition rules: Decide whether an individual step runs. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Give a distinct international hiring org its own organization-specific definition rather than one default full of conditionally skipped steps. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: View Business Process Definition, Edit Definition, Copy Definition, and Set as Completion. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] All approvals and reviews must precede the completion step, though later To Dos may remain. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
