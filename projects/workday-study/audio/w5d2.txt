Week 5, day 2. business process definitions. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Update business process Definitions, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Choose between default, organization-specific, and rule-based definitions, and set the completion step. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] Workday delivers business process types; you configure definitions. Workday recommends one default definition because it is easiest to maintain. Use an organization-specific definition when an organization's steps differ greatly; it covers that organization and its subordinates. Use rule-based definitions when the same condition rule would sit on three or more steps, or when the path depends on who initiates or where workers are. Each rule-based definition has one condition rule, which Workday evaluates once, at initiation, to pick the definition. If no rule is met, Workday uses the organization-specific definition for the organization or its nearest superior, then the default. Definitions with errors or that are inactive are skipped. The completion step commits the data and sets Successfully Completed. A process has only one, and all approvals and reviews must come before it or be it. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Default: Recommended and easiest to maintain. [[slnc 700]] Organization-specific: Own steps for an organization whose flow differs greatly. [[slnc 700]] Rule-based: One condition rule, checked once at initiation, picks the definition. [[slnc 700]] Completion step: Commits data, and approvals and reviews come first. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Hires in three countries follow different approval paths, and the same location rule sits on four steps of the default. Replace those step conditions with three rule-based definitions, one per location rule. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Edit Definition, Inactivate Business Process Definition, Set as Completion, and Business Process Definitions for Business Object. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] A rule-based definition uses exactly one condition rule (it can nest conditions) and only fields that have a value at initiation. Approvals and reviews never come after the completion step. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check. I'll read each question and the choices, then pause so you can answer out loud. [[slnc 1000]] Question one.
