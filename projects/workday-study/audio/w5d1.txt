Week 5, day 1. business process framework. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is business process Terminology  and  Framework, in the Business Processes domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Understand type, definition, event, steps, routing, conditions, and security. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] A business process type is the event category; a definition is its configured workflow; an event is one transaction running through that definition. Definitions specify steps, order, responsible security groups, conditions, notifications, and completion. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Type: Event category. [[slnc 700]] Definition: Configured workflow. [[slnc 700]] Event: One running transaction. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Two hires use the same Hire definition but create separate events routed for their own subjects and organizations.
