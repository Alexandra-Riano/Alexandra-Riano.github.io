[[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Master the exam-heavy distinction between control and flexibility. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Management Options. [[slnc 500]] Job Management emphasizes speed and flexibility. Restrictions apply across the supervisory organization, there is no system-imposed position count, and the job instance ends when the worker leaves. Reporting focuses on current headcount. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Flexible or high-volume staffing. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Supervisory organization. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: After restrictions are set, changing the staffing model is no longer a simple edit. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Position Management Options. [[slnc 500]] A position exists independently from its incumbent. Restrictions apply to an individual unfilled position, an available position is required before hire, and the position can remain vacant after the worker leaves. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Individual seat and vacancy control. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Individual position. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Do not choose Job Management when the scenario requires persistent vacancy or position-level history. [[slnc 1300]] Two retrieval questions to finish.
