Today is a review session. Score 80% or higher and explain when a hybrid hierarchy makes sense. [[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Master the exam-heavy distinction between control and flexibility. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Management Options. [[slnc 500]] Job Management emphasizes speed and flexibility. No open position is required to hire. Hiring restrictions are set on the supervisory organization and apply to every job in it; there is no system-imposed limit on jobs, and the job ends when the worker leaves. Reporting covers current headcount only, so control relies on hire, contract, and transfer workflows. Restriction criteria are the same for both models: job family, job profile, location, worker type, worker sub type, time type, or No Job Restrictions (you must choose at least one). Edit Tenant Setup - H C M can allow users to override restrictions during staffing events; the restriction values still default in. Subordinate organizations inherit their parent's staffing model, but each can be changed, so one hierarchy can mix models. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Flexible or high-volume staffing. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Supervisory organization. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Edit Staffing Model works in either direction only until positions exist (Position Management) or hiring restrictions are set (Job Management). After that the task is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Position Management Options. [[slnc 500]] A position exists independently from its incumbent. An open, approved position is required to hire, promote, demote, or transfer a worker. Restrictions apply to an individual position, and a position stays open until it is filled or manually closed. You can report on open and filled positions and on position history, assign company, cost center, and region to unfilled positions, and move positions between organizations. [[slnc 700]] Best fit: Individual seat and vacancy control. [[slnc 500]] Restriction level: Individual position. [[slnc 500]] Changing the model: Edit Staffing Model, only before positions or restrictions exist. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Do not choose Job Management when the scenario requires persistent vacancy or position-level history. Once positions are created, Edit Staffing Model is no longer available, so choose the model before staffing begins. [[slnc 1300]] Two retrieval questions to finish. [[slnc 800]] Question one.
