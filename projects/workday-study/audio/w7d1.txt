Week 7, day 1. Standard reports. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Identify Standard Reports, in the Reporting domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Use Workday-delivered reports and understand purpose, type, security, and scheduling. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] The Workday Standard Reports report lists every delivered report with its category, description, type (XpressO or Report Writer), whether it is schedulable, and its securing domain. You need the Custom Report Administration domain to run it, and seeing an entry does not mean you can run the report. Only Report Writer reports can be copied, which needs the Custom Report Creation domain. Common standard reports by purpose: plan and configure business processes with Business Process Configuration Options; see which definition an organization uses with Business Process Definitions for Business Object; track organization activity with Reorganizations and Organization Exception Audit; view job profiles with Job Catalog and Job Family. Report Writer report types are Simple, Advanced, Matrix, Composite, Search, nBox, Trending, and Transposed. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Run delivered: Use when it meets the need. [[slnc 700]] Copy or custom: Use when requirements differ. [[slnc 700]] Plan business processes: Business Process Configuration Options. [[slnc 700]] Track organization activity: Reorganizations, Organization Exception Audit. [[slnc 700]] View job profiles: Job Catalog, Job Family. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Workday Standard Reports, Copy Standard Report to Custom Report, Business Process Configuration Options, Reorganizations, Job Catalog, and Hide Workday Delivered Report.
