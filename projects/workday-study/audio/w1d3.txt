Week 1, day 3. Connect the catalog. [[slnc 1200]] Today is a review session. Compare profile, family, family group, job, and position. [[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Build the language and structure that every later topic depends on. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Families  and  Groups. [[slnc 500]] A job profile may belong to one or more job families; a family can belong to a family group. Families can support prompt filters, reports, Recruiting categories, condition rules, and eligibility rules. [[slnc 700]] Job family: Groups related profiles. [[slnc 500]] Job family group: Groups families into a broader catalog layer. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: A family classifies work; it is not a supervisory organization or staffing model. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Create  and  Edit Job Profiles. [[slnc 500]] Job profiles describe general work characteristics. Normal required fields are name, job code, and effective date unless job-code generation is configured. Optional fields include title, management level, classifications, pay rate type, compensation grade, qualifications, country restriction, and description. [[slnc 700]] Job profile: Reusable work definition. [[slnc 500]] Job or position: Staffable instance in an organization. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: A profile is not a worker and is not the same as a position. Effective-dated edits can affect staffing, eligibility, and reporting.
