Week 1, day 3. Connect the catalog. [[slnc 1200]] Today is a review session. Compare profile, family, family group, job, and position. [[slnc 1000]] This week's goal: Build the language and structure that every later topic depends on. [[slnc 1200]] Recap: Job Families  and  Groups. [[slnc 500]] A job profile may belong to one or more job families; a family can belong to a family group. Families can support prompt filters, reports, Recruiting categories, condition rules, and eligibility rules. [[slnc 700]] Job family: Groups related profiles. [[slnc 500]] Job family group: Groups families into a broader catalog layer. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: A family classifies work; it is not a supervisory organization or staffing model. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Create  and  Edit Job Profiles. [[slnc 500]] Job profiles describe general work characteristics. Normal required fields are name, job code, and effective date unless job-code generation is configured. Optional fields include title, management level, classifications, pay rate type, compensation grade, qualifications, country restriction, and description. [[slnc 700]] Job profile: Reusable work definition. [[slnc 500]] Job or position: Staffable instance in an organization. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: A profile is not a worker and is not the same as a position. Effective-dated edits can affect staffing, eligibility, and reporting. [[slnc 1300]] Recap: Navigation, Search  and  Tenants. [[slnc 500]] A tenant is one instance of Workday. Production holds live data; Sandbox is a copy for testing; Sandbox Preview adds the next release. Workday metadata cannot change; your configuration and data are tenanted data. Feature releases come twice a year, and service updates arrive weekly. Search shows predictive results after three characters and treats each term separately; Exact Match finds IDs and email addresses. Search prefixes limit results to one object type, for example B P colon for business process definitions, event colon for events, sup: for supervisory organizations, org: for organizations, and worker: for workers; type ? to list all prefixes. Related Actions show what you can do with an object, based on your security. Proxy (Start Proxy) lets testers act as another user, but only outside Production. The Workday Touchpoints Kit shows data flows and configuration relationships across products. [[slnc 700]] Production versus Sandbox: Live data versus a test copy. [[slnc 500]] B P colon versus event colon: Definitions versus running events. [[slnc 500]] Related Actions: What you can do with an object. [[slnc 500]] Proxy: Testing as another user, never in Production. [[slnc 500]] Remember the trap: Proxy access policies apply to non-production tenants only, and only unconstrained security groups can be given proxy access.
