Week 4, day 2. Organization hierarchies. [[slnc 1200]] Today's objective is Organization Hierarchies, in the Organizations domain. [[slnc 600]] In one line: Roll up organizations of one type for reporting, and use reorganization events to date changes. [[slnc 1200]] Let's start with the mental model. [[slnc 500]] An organization hierarchy rolls up organizations of its own type for reporting: a cost center hierarchy holds only cost centers, a location hierarchy only locations. Hierarchies support security and the inheritance of roles down the hierarchy. Transactions are recorded on organizations, never on the hierarchy; costs roll up automatically. One organization can sit in several hierarchies, and hierarchies can include other hierarchies. Assign roles such as HR Partner (by location) on a location hierarchy, not on individual locations; workers join a location hierarchy by being hired or contracted with an included location. Creating or changing organizations and hierarchies uses a reorganization event, which groups activities under one common effective date. Track them with the Reorganizations report. [[slnc 1200]] Now, the distinctions you need to know. [[slnc 500]] Hierarchy: Rollup of one organization type for reporting and role inheritance. [[slnc 700]] Reorganization event colon Groups changes under one effective date. [[slnc 700]] Organization: Where transactions are recorded. [[slnc 700]]  [[slnc 600]] Here's how it shows up at work. [[slnc 500]] Roll three cost centers into an IT Services cost center hierarchy. Costs are charged to each cost center and roll up automatically; the management chain does not change. [[slnc 1200]] Task and report names to recognize: Create Cost Center Hierarchy, Create Location Hierarchy, Reorganization, then Assign Included Organizations, Reorganization, then Assign Locations, Create Reorganization, and Reorganizations report. [[slnc 1200]] And the exam trap to watch for. [[slnc 500]] A hierarchy never stores transactions. Location-based roles go on a location hierarchy, not on a single location. [[slnc 1500]] Quick check.
